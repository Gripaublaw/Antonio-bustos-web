Resum

Antonio Bustos was born in Córdoba, in southern Spain. At seventeen he
experienced what flamenco artists call "the calling": he fell in love with
flamenco and, above all, with the guitar. His mother had been a dancer at the
Apollo Theatre in Barcelona — she had once danced for King Hussein of Jordan —
and she was a decisive influence, playing and dancing to Spanish classical music
at home.

He was fortunate to train with great masters such as Manuel Cañizares and Manuel
Granados, and completed his studies with the latter at the Professional
Conservatory of the Liceu in Barcelona. He then toured all over Europe with the
sextet Tenderete Flamenco.

Determined to compose his own music, he settled in Cadaqués, a bohemian village
in the north-east of the peninsula, where he composed his first album, "Casa en
las Nubes". He is currently working on the second, "Renacer", which brings
drawing and poetry into original compositions that cross cultures from the north
and south of Spain: a rondeña from Málaga and a habanera from Cadaqués meet in a
single piece.

He collaborates with Antonia Peris, a contemporary dancer awarded at a
prestigious festival in Mexico. With her he works on "Sintonia", a production
that Peris composes and directs, in which Antonio performs as guitarist and
actor: the first flamenco guitarist to play a theatrical role in a production of
this kind.

The new album also welcomes Sarah Thomson, pianist and violinist from the London
Conservatory. It is a work that excites him particularly, because it represents
a breath of fresh air: a fusion of theatre, music, drawing and poetry within the
field of flamenco.
